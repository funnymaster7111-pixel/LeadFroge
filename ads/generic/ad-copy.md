### Ad 3 video version: 30-second patient testimonial script

**Format:** talking head filmed on a phone, vertical 9:16. One patient, natural light (face a window),
quiet room in the clinic or at the patient's home. No script for the patient to read. The lines below
are **interview prompts**. Ask the question off-camera and let the patient answer in their own words.
Use their real answers only. Don't hand them lines.

| Time | What's on screen | Audio / what to capture | On-screen text (captions on) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0–3s: Hook** | Tight shot, patient already mid-sentence, looking just off-camera | Open on the patient's strongest real line about what changed for them. Pick it in the edit, never write it. *Prompt to get it: "If a friend asked you about this program, what's the first thing you'd tell them?"* | `[Patient Name], Your City` |
| **3–10s: Before** | Same shot | Where they were before and what they'd already tried. *Prompts: "What made you start looking for help?" · "Had you tried other programs or apps before? How did that go?"* | `Tried online programs before?` *(only if they say they did)* |
| **10–20s: The difference** | Optional 1–2s B-roll: patient walking into the clinic, chatting with Dr. [Name] or staff (with consent) | What it's like having a local doctor and team. *Prompts: "What's it like working with Dr. [Name] in person?" · "What do your check-ins look like?" · "How does it feel having a team here in town?"* | `A real doctor in Your City` |
| **20–25s: Today** | Back to patient, smiling / relaxed | How they feel now, in their words: energy, confidence, daily life. Don't ask for pounds or numbers. *Prompt: "How is day-to-day life different now?"* | — |
| **25–30s: CTA** | End card: navy background, Your Clinic Name, Your City | Voiceover (staff, or the patient if they're happy to): **"Want to see if it's right for you? Book a free consultation with Dr. [Name] at Your Clinic Name, right here in Your City."** | `Book your free consult` · `Individual results vary.` |

**Filming checklist**
- Signed media consent from the patient, covering paid social ads
- Phone at eye level, about an arm's length away, rear camera, 4K/30 if available
- Record 5–10 minutes of conversation and cut the best 30 seconds. Don't do take after take of one line
- No before/after photos, no scales or tape measures, no shirtless or "problem area" shots
- Don't name or show the medication (no pens, vials, or boxes on camera)
- Burn in captions, since most people watch with sound off

## Compliance notes for every ad

These are general notes, not legal advice. The clinic should check with its own counsel and Meta's current policies.

- **Meta weight-loss rules:** target ages 18+. No before/after images. Don't imply anything negative
  about the viewer's body ("Hate your belly?"). The copy above sets the doctor against online programs, never against the person.
- **Prescription drugs:** Meta generally requires advertisers who promote prescription drugs to be
  certified (e.g. through LegitScript). Keeping medication generic ("FDA-approved medication options")
  is safer copy, but the clinic should still confirm whether it needs certification.
- **Testimonials:** real patients only, with written consent, and "Individual results vary" on any
  testimonial ad. Check HIPAA rules before using any patient's name or likeness.
- **Offers:** spell out the real terms of any free period, and run "limited" / "this month only" claims only while they're true.
