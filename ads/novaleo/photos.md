# Novaleo Weight & Wellness: photo sources

**Status: no photos downloaded yet.** The build session's network policy blocked novaweightandwellness.com, facebook.com, instagram.com, google.com, images.unsplash.com and images.pexels.com, so nothing has been pulled. Each slot below says what goes in it and where it comes from. Drop the file into `photos/` with the slot filename, then run the build (see `ads/README.md`).

| `office-*.jpg` | Spare (video B-roll, future ads) | Office exterior / treatment room | novaweightandwellness.com (about page), else Facebook/Google profile | REAL |

Pick stock photos with: a waist-up or three-quarter shot, a genuine smile, eyes toward the camera, a plain or simple background (cleaner cutout), portrait orientation, at least 1500px tall. Avoid before/after poses, measuring tapes and scales (Meta's personal-health ad policy).

`*-cut.png` files are generated by `build/cutout.py` (rembg, u2net_human_seg model). Don't edit them by hand.
