### Ad 3, video version: 30-second talking-head script

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, filmed on a phone, natural light in the Shaperz consult room or at the front entrance with the 111th St. sign in view. A real patient talks straight to camera. Burn in captions, because most people watch with the sound off.

**Important:** The patient's lines below are **interview prompts, not lines to read**. Ask each question, let them answer in their own words, and cut the best 4–6 seconds of each answer. Do not script or rewrite what they say.

| Time | On screen | Audio | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | Close-up of the patient smiling, at shoulder height or above | Patient introduces themself. *Prompt: "Tell us your first name and what part of Chicago you're from."* | **[First name], [neighborhood]** |
| 0:03–0:10 | Patient talking | *Prompt: "Why did you choose a local clinic over an online program?"* | **Why a local clinic?** |
| 0:10–0:18 | Patient talking; B-roll cutaway: the nurse practitioner greeting them at the door, then the two of them talking at the consult table | *Prompt: "What was your first visit like?"* | **A 60-minute first visit** |
| 0:18–0:24 | Patient talking | *Prompt: "How would you describe the care you get here?"* (Steer toward the experience, not numbers.) | **Nurse Practitioner-led care** |
| 0:24–0:30 | End card: logo on a blush-to-white background with a thin gold border, $249 badge, address | Voiceover (staff): "New patients get their first month for $249, with labs, a 3D body scan, and a meal plan included. Shaperz Elite, on 111th Street. Get ready, get set, get healthy." | **$249 First Month · New Patients** · 3135 W. 111th St · **Book Now** · *Get ready, get set, get healthy* (script) |

**Rules for filming and editing (Meta policy):**
- No scales, measuring tapes, mirrors, body close-ups, or before/after shots. Keep the frame at chest height or above.
- Cut any answer that mentions a drug or brand name, a number of pounds or sizes, or how they felt about their body "before".
- Cut any line that promises results (for example, "you'll lose…").
- Small lower-third text for the whole video: "Real patient. Individual experiences vary."
- Have the patient sign a written testimonial and likeness release before the video runs.
